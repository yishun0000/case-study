Master The Brew

Simple, precise steps to elevate your daily home brewing ritual

01

Measure Accurately

Use a digital scale. We recommend a 1:15 coffee-to-water ratio for pour-over, or 2g of tea leaves per 200ml water.

02

Water Temperature

Temperature matters! Brew coffee around 92°C-96°C, and delicate green tea between 70°C-80°C.

03

Timing & Patience

Steep green tea for 2-3 minutes. Allow pour-over coffee to brew completely within 3.5 minutes for optimal taste.
