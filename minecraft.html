An Endless Sandbox World

Minecraft is a game about placing blocks and going on adventures. There are no rules holding you back. Whether you gather resources to test your limits in Survival mode, or construct architectural wonders with unlimited blocks in Creative mode, the world is entirely yours to shape.

Day One Survival

Follow these three basic steps to survive your first dangerous night

01

Punch Wood

Break nearby trees to get wood, craft a Crafting Table, and make wooden tools to kickstart your progression.

02

Build a Shelter

Dig a cave or build a quick hut before sunset to keep Creepers and Zombies from attacking you at night.

03

Mine Ores & Make Torches

Find coal and iron, craft torches for light, upgrade your gear to iron, and gear up for deeper cave exploration.
